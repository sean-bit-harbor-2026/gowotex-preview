关于格物泰克ABOUT GOWOTEX

国际化商务服务机构
承继二十余年国际会议积淀 · 连接中国与世界创新资源An International Business Services Firm
20+ Years of Conference Legacy Connecting Global Innovation with China

格物泰克商务会展（大连）有限公司（Gowotex）是一家以国际高端会议与展览为平台，集商务服务、高端人才引进与跨境科技项目对接于一体的国际化商务服务机构。创始人与核心管理团队深耕国际高端会议与展览领域二十余年，曾主导运营多个国际品牌会议平台，积累了覆盖全球的专家资源与一站式全流程服务能力；公司在此基础上整合组建，全面承继多年积淀的业务、核心资源与品牌资产，坚持“全球视野 · 服务本土”理念，致力于打造中国与世界创新资源对接的核心平台，为客户提供高效、便捷、具有国际影响力的商务对接服务。Gowotex Business Exhibition (Dalian) Co., Ltd. is an international business services firm built on high-end international conferences and exhibitions, integrating business services, high-level talent introduction and cross-border technology & project matching. The founders and core management team have been engaged in international conferences and exhibitions for over 20 years, leading multiple international conference brands and building a global expert network with one-stop, full-cycle service capabilities. Founded on this legacy, Gowotex fully inherits the business, core resources and brand assets accumulated over two decades. Guided by the philosophy of “Global Vision · Serving Local Growth”, we are committed to becoming a core platform connecting China with the world’s innovation resources.

业务体系BUSINESS SYSTEMS

业务体系 · 共享同一国际专家资源与作业体系Business Systems on One Shared Platform

格物泰克业务围绕国家战略性新兴产业和未来产业方向，以国际会议为纽带，形成业务体系协同的全链条服务生态：以会聚人、以人引才、以才招商、以商成项，各体系共享国际专家资源与作业体系，相互导流、协同增强，并可面向政府、企业及行业组织提供会议、展览、人才、项目一体化的定制化解决方案。Gowotex’s business is built around China’s strategic emerging and future industries. With international conferences as the connecting link, our business systems form a coordinated, full-chain service ecosystem — gathering talent through conferences, attracting talent to regions, turning talent into investment, and turning deals into projects. All systems share the same global expert resources and delivery framework, channel leads to one another and reinforce each other, and can serve governments, enterprises and industry organizations with integrated, customized solutions across conferences, exhibitions, talent and projects.

01🎪

国际会议品牌International Conference Brands

自主创办及联合主办大型高端国际品牌会议，主办、承办各类专业展览会，提供选题立项、日程策划、专家委员会组建、大会主席邀请、招展招商、会务执行与会后总结全流程服务，并组织政府、园区及企事业单位组团参加境内外国际会议与展览，协助企业拓展海外市场。We create and co-host high-end international conference brands and organize professional exhibitions, delivering the full cycle — topic selection, agenda design, program-committee formation, conference chair recruitment, exhibitor and sponsor development, on-site execution and post-event review. We also organize government, park and enterprise delegations to attend international conferences and exhibitions in China and abroad, helping companies expand into overseas markets.

服务：政府 / 学术机构 / 企业For: Government · Academia · Enterprises
02🎓

国际人才引进服务Global Talent Acquisition

依托自有国际专家数据库，为政府、高校、科研院所及企业开展海外高层次人才寻访、评估与引进落地服务，引进国际高端专家、客座教授与科研团队，推动学术研究与科技创新。Based on our proprietary global expert database, we source, evaluate and onboard overseas high-level talent for governments, universities, research institutes and enterprises — bringing in distinguished international experts, visiting professors and research teams to advance academic research and technological innovation.

服务：政府 / 高校 / 科研院所 / 企业For: Government · Universities · Research Institutes · Enterprises
03📡

会议情报与行业媒体Conference Intelligence & Media

沉淀历届会议议题、议程、嘉宾与产业知识，运营国际人才智库，提供全球会议日历、行业资讯与研究简报等情报产品与决策咨询服务，并为会展业上下游（场馆、搭建、酒店、差旅、翻译、技术服务等）提供信息与广告增值服务。We turn the themes, agendas, speakers and industry knowledge of past conferences into searchable intelligence — operating an international talent think tank and delivering conference calendars, industry news, research briefings and decision-support advisory services. We also provide information and advertising value-added services to the MICE supply chain, including venues, stand construction, hotels, travel, translation and technical services.

服务：会展产业链 / 主办方For: MICE Supply Chain · Organizers
04📝

会议策划服务（B2B）Conference Planning (B2B)

面向企业、行业协会与机构提供国际会议策划，输出议题框架、议程编排、嘉宾策略与会议文案，承接各类机构会议、论坛、节庆活动的策划与执行，提供从策划到执行的一站式顾问服务。For enterprises, industry associations and institutions we deliver international conference planning — theme frameworks, agenda programming, speaker strategy and conference copywriting — and take on the planning and execution of institutional meetings, forums and festival events, as a one-stop advisory service from concept to delivery.

服务：企业 / 行业协会For: Enterprises · Industry Associations
05⚙️

会议全案代运营Full-Cycle Conference Operations

承接主办方委托的会议全案代运营，将注册、招展、嘉宾、内容、现场与展后运营接入公司标准化作业体系，协办行业学会、协会年会，助力主办方扩大规模与影响力。We take on full-cycle conference operations on behalf of organizers, plugging registration, exhibitor development, speakers, content, on-site and post-event operations into our standardized delivery system. We co-organize annual meetings of academic societies and industry associations, helping organizers grow their scale and influence.

服务：主办方 / 学会协会For: Organizers · Societies & Associations
06🏟️

B2B/B2G 会议与展中会承办B2B/B2G Conferences & Expo Forums

承接企业级（B2B）与政府级（B2G）会议承办，尤其擅长大型博览会与行业展会中的“展中会”——在主展流量场内组织专题论坛、技术研讨、新品发布与供需对接会，以“展的流量 × 专业的会”提升展会内容品质与客商黏性。We deliver enterprise-level (B2B) and government-level (B2G) conferences, with particular strength in “conferences within exhibitions” — organizing themed forums, technology seminars, product launches and buyer–supplier matchmaking inside major expos and trade shows. Expo traffic multiplied by conference expertise raises content quality and keeps exhibitors and buyers engaged.

服务：大型展会 / 企业 / 政府For: Expo Groups · Enterprises · Government
07🎤

演讲局与城市会展顾问服务Speakers Bureau & City MICE Advisory

为各类会议、展览、节庆及学术交流活动招募国际高端专家，包括诺奖大师、国外院士、世界 500 强企业高管及国际知名人士；为城市会展局与商务部门提供国际会议申办研判与申报、会展规划与对标分析、展会评估与政策申报辅导、城市会奖资源与目的地推介等顾问服务，协助城市引入品牌会议，串联招商与引才。We recruit world-class speakers for conferences, exhibitions, festivals and academic exchanges — Nobel laureates, foreign academicians, Fortune 500 executives and internationally renowned figures. For city convention bureaus and commerce departments we provide bid research and application support, MICE planning and benchmarking, event evaluation and policy-application guidance, and promotion of city MICE resources and destinations — helping cities attract brand conferences and linking investment attraction with talent recruitment.

服务：城市会展局 / 商务部门 / 主办方For: Convention Bureaus · Commerce Departments · Organizers
08🏛️

政府招商服务Investment Attraction Services

以会招商，依托国际专家与企业家网络，结合地方产业方向绘制产业链招商图谱，组织招商型会议与政企对接，配合地方政府、产业园区与孵化器推动产业项目与创业团队落地，吸纳海外学子回国创业。Using conferences to attract investment, we map industrial chains with our global expert and entrepreneur network, organize investment-focused conferences and government–business matchmaking, and work with local governments, industrial parks and incubators to land industrial projects and startup teams — including attracting overseas Chinese talent back home to start businesses.

服务：地方政府 / 园区 / 孵化器For: Local Government · Parks · Incubators
09🔗

国际项目对接服务Global Project Matching

开展中外技术转移、产学研合作、商务拓展与联合实验室等项目的撮合服务，为企业提供产品展示、市场推广与客户对接，通过国际会议运作与海外资源积累，推动国际需求与国内产业、资本精准对接。We match Chinese and international partners on technology transfer, industry–academia–research collaboration, business development and joint laboratories, and support companies with product showcases, market promotion and customer introductions. Through conference operations and accumulated overseas resources, we connect global demand precisely with domestic industry and capital.

服务：企业 / 高校院所 / 园区For: Enterprises · Academia · Industrial Parks
